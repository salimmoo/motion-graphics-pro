# Reference: taste, composition, typography

Part of the `motion-graphics` skill. Read when: the style stage (chapter 0 items 2-4) and composing frames.

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md v2.3 (1.1 Taste, 1.2 Composition and typography); the old chapter numbers are kept, so cross-references such as "chapter 6.4" or "T9a" can still be found in the reference files (the chapter map is in SKILL.md, References section).

## 1.1 Taste
- A palette of 3-6 colours per piece. Pattern: 1 main hue + 1 dark + 1 accent (lime/navy; red/black/white), or pastel off-white #F3F3F3 + 2-3 blues.
- A locked 3-colour sequence: light background, dark silhouette, 1 warm accent only for rims and details (about 5-10% of the area, estimate). Myth example: #7FB9CB / #0D2330 / #E8694A.
- A saturated cool subject (#2B3BD6) on a warm 2-stop vertical gradient (#F2875E to #EBA56E); shadows towards #0A0A1E.
- 8-9 colours only when everything is flat with a uniform dark outline of 1.5-2 px at 1080p (estimate), strokes not scaled.
- Each scene has its own solid background colour, a soft radial glow behind the subject, and a floor one step darker.
- Depth through colour: the distance lighter and less saturated (towards the sky colour), the foreground dark and blurred.
- Finish with light grain/noise or rough edges of 1-3 px; texture clipped inside each shape, not over the whole frame; dry-brush edge = path point jitter of 1-2 px. Light sources get a blurred copy on top (glow).
- Ending: tint the whole frame (for example lilac #A6B4F0), show the title, hold for about 3 s.

## 1.2 Composition and typography
- One subject in the middle with wide empty space; cinematic: the subject on the right third, vertical elements (tree trunks) as rhythm, a dark foreground cutting the bottom edge. Aspect ratio 16:9, 2.67:1 or 4:3.
- A 4:5 poster crop inside the 16:9 frame with cream margins (#F4F0EA); full-bleed during action, back to the poster for the title.
- Symmetry for hero shots: one-point perspective or a 4/8-fold kaleidoscope.
- Typography: narration in a small lowercase geometric sans; keywords in an ultra-condensed display face; titles bold extended caps; captions thin with very wide tracking (+300-400, estimate); collage style = a different font per word; credits small with wide tracking.
