# Reference: learning sources

Part of the `motion-graphics` skill. Read when: looking for the origin of a rule or an AE technique.

Moved verbatim from SKILL.md v2.3 (1. Sources); the old chapter numbers are kept, so cross-references such as "chapter 6.4" or "T9a" can still be found in the reference files (the chapter map is in SKILL.md, References section).

## 1. Sources
- Adrianne Walujo (Adobe APAC): separate named layers; CC Bend It for leaves; loopOut(); Wave Warp for water; night variant = swap colours, keep the layers.
- Dody Priyatmono: anchor at the joint (Pan Behind); leg rotation 0-12-24 f; Easy Ease; Puppet Pin on knees/elbows; parent null + squash; Time Remap loopOut("cycle"); background moving the opposite way; Gaussian Blur for depth.
- Jess (Envato) and Audrey (School of Motion): round joints, rotation anchor at the centre of the joint circle (R), parenting (face > head > neck > torso; pupil > eye white; fingers > palm > forearm > upper arm > torso), big motion first, then detail, then secondary. Hands and mouths = swap sets.
- Ben Marriott: blocking hold keys -> easing -> secondary; Graph Editor anticipation; Posterize Time 12 fps (on twos); Turbulent Displace boil (random seed time*4); legs as shape paths + a knee point; retime with Alt-drag; rough pass of lines + circles; noise 8% + wiggle 1.5 px 6x/second. Burnt Toast: action 20 f, path keys every frame, 3-shape shading, 1-frame smear.
- AE Puppet Pin: animate only the pins that need it; loopOut("pingpong"); different part durations and reversed direction (Time-Reverse) to avoid unison.
- Swaying wave: mask the free part + Wave Warp, pin the attached edge.
- SonduckFilm (Cinematic Motion Graphic Stories): 2D layers lit in 3D space, wind wave warp, particles, 360 orbit through a null, DOF + vignette + noise.
- edwin rowin (Jammin' Out): rig from the hips without IK, on twos/fours, head turn with arc + hold, motion never stops completely, edge roughness by distance.
- Nijat Ibrahimli (Lip Sync): 12 mouth shapes, time remap hold.
- Taste references batch 1: IKEA Together (allkustom), GATE (azma), define. (aflow), "us" / Tarot / am not cute anymor. (y6richo), Cupid (wanezv), 결정 (Rafi Rizky).
- Taste references batch 2: Re:bake and I'M LOST. (seven), Bohdan Martovskyi Showreel 2025, Cloudwalk Showreel 2023, Glam (Dan Stein), Myth Showreel 2021.
- Standard principles (written reference animation-principles): numbers in chapter 2.
- Internal motion study (approved): puppet mesh + IK rig for waving, a silat side kick, jumping, walking, sitting; rig from traced artwork (DeformSpace) for idle, waving, side-view walk, turnaround (chapters 3.1, 4, 4.1).
- Vector background research (test project, principles): Clip Studio "Flat Design Illustration 6 Styles" and "How to paint: Trees"; BINUS DKV "Backgrounds that Breathe"; Creative Bloq "How to depict forest light" and "Inject great depth into flat colours"; Painting Lessons with Marla "Color in Light and Shadow"; Zoung Art "Painting in the style of Sleeping Beauty" (Eyvind Earle); Animation Obsessive "What Kazuo Oga thinks about" (chapter 5.1).
